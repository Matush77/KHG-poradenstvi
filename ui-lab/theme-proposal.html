The one finding that matters most

In 2026 the purple gradient is no longer the main AI look. Critics now name serif headings + warm cream + terracotta as the new default that models reach for. Our site is Playfair Display + cream #FAF8F5 + coral #E8927C — exactly that cluster — on top of Inter, which is the other most-cited default.

None of that is wrong by itself; the problem is when all of them stack with no reason. The fix is not to chase another trend, but to take every choice from something true about this practice. We have one: the blog illustrations you like — paper, three blues, a coral sun. This proposal takes the palette from them, swaps the default serif (Playfair) for one picked to match the art (Fraunces, soft), and removes decoration that carries no meaning.

Five rules for the new theme

1. Take it from the illustrations

Every colour on the site comes from the blog art: warm paper, pale sky, two blues, navy, and the coral sun. Nothing else.

2. Two families, each with one job

Headings in Fraunces (soft, 500), everything else in Inter at 400 / 500 / 600. Owner, 12 Nov: Inter alone had no character. Buttons, menu, labels and FAQ stay Inter. Logo: the owner’s coral heart + name in Fraunces + role in Inter caps, as text, no image.

3. Tone, not boxes

Group things with a background band and space. A white card only where something really is a separate object (a review, a price, a booking option). No shadows on cards.

4. Coral means "act"

Coral is for booking buttons and at most one small accent per screen. No coral underlines, bars or gradients.

5. Kristýna's words, not a template's

Cut sentences any therapist could sign. Prefer one concrete fact (price, place, method, who it's for) over a reassuring phrase.

And: keep what already works

Real portrait and room, up-front prices, credentials, 17 px text, 66-character lines, no trackers, coral booking buttons.

Typography

7 sizes, two families

Down from 40 styles and 6 weights. Headings are Fraunces 500 (soft) with slightly tight spacing, sentence case, no underline; Fraunces is one 500 file, so no heading asks for another weight. Everything else is Inter 400 / 500 / 600. Playfair (about 40 kB) goes, Fraunces (30 kB, Czech subset) comes: the same one font swap as today.

Layout system

Every part is one of six things

Measured on the lab homepage at 375 px and 1157 px. Before this, section titles started at the left edge while the blocks under them were centred at five different widths (700, 800, 900, 1000, 1078 px), gaps between blocks ranged 32–96 px, and card padding was 36 px, off the spacing scale. These rules replace that.

1. Parts

Bandwhole section
Paper or sky, alternating. Navy only for the footer. All 10 homepage sections.
Rowsspace, no line
No box and no line between items (owner, 13 Nov): each row is told apart by its heading and the space around it. One-line facts close up into a plain list (4 px rows, navy 500). Pro koho, kroky, Praktické informace, FAQ, Kontakt, facts in cards. Exception: link rows with an arrow keep their hairlines (profily online, related articles).
Cardwhite, r12
A separate object: white, 1 px line on paper (none on sky), radius 12, no shadow. Služba, ČAP, Publikace, the format cards (tinted sky / peach instead of white), Ceník prices.
Panelr24
The few large, coloured or featured blocks. Booking panel (peach, everywhere), review cards, the booking window.
Photor12
Real photos only, radius 12, sized to fit the layout. Ordinace (large + 3 on desktop, swipe row on phones). The portrait keeps its rounded blob shape, still (no animation), as the one exception.
Actionbutton / link
One button style for booking (below). Everything else is a text link: underline or ↗ for external. No ghost or second-colour button for booking. Blog and quizzes only: non-booking actions (start, next, back) are navy, solid for the main step and outline for the rest.

4. Width and alignment

  • Everything in a section starts at the same left edge as its title. Only the hero and the section titles centre, and only on phones; paragraphs, cards, panels and buttons inside sections are always left-aligned (centred multi-line text is harder to read).
  • Blocks are always full width on every screen; a narrow block next to an empty half reads as unfinished. On desktop a block uses its own columns instead: Pro koho rows put the title left and the text right, topics run in 2 columns, the About text is one column (no credential summaries, owner 13 Nov; the IVF group shows above "Číst dál"), Kontakt rows have a 14rem label column. The service card is one column. Long text stops at 66 characters per line (the reading measure), wherever it sits.

5. Order: the questions a first-time client asks

Czech research names price, waiting and shame as the reasons people don't start; clients check fit, credentials and reviews before booking. The homepage answers in that order: Hero (who, online or Ostrava, booking) → O mně (who is she? owner, 12 Nov: first) → Pro koho (is this for me?) → Recenze (do others trust her?) → Služby (what, how much; the price lives here, not in the hero) (what, how much) → Jak probíhá → Osobní a online → FAQ → Členství → Kontakt. Bands alternate paper / sky in this order: paper for Hero, Pro koho, Služby, Osobní a online, Členství; sky for O mně, Recenze, Jak probíhá, FAQ, Kontakt.

6. Button, review colours, motion

  • One booking button: coral with navy text (4.99 : 1; white on this coral is 2.38 : 1 and fails WCAG even for large text), pill, 18 px / 600. Two sizes: compact 44 px in the header; 56 px / 18 px text in the page, full column width on phones (measured 12 Nov: Hedepy hero button 335×76 on a 375 px phone, Terapie.cz 311×38, live site 168×55; ours at 156×52 read as too small). Repeating the same primary action is allowed; a different-looking button for it is not. Hover darkens nothing and moves nothing: it switches to the light coral.
  • Phone menu and booking window use the same parts. Menu: paper, full height, links as rows at lead size (no lines), the page-size booking button last; it slides in with transform only (200 ms, none with reduced motion). Booking window: a paper panel (r24, panel padding, the one allowed shadow), title left at h3, options stacked in one column; the title leads with the format ("Online", "Osobně v Ostravě"), then one line and a navy "→", no separate labels. Format tints: online is always sky, in person always peach, here and on the Ceník cards. Both are tinted and of equal weight: one white card beside a tinted one reads as "already selected". On tints, meta text is slate (mute is only 4.3 : 1 on peach). Hover (desktop, mouse only): a format card that is one action (booking option, Ceník price card) is clickable as a whole; on hover its tint deepens one step (sky #DCE6EE, peach #F2DBCC), the arrow moves 4 px right or the button turns light coral. 150 ms, no movement of the card, none with reduced motion.
  • Facts inside a card are plain, not a box. No lines, fill or radius; columns sit apart by the gap; label 15 / 500 mute, value navy 600 (Fraunces 500 when it is a price or length), detail 15 / 400 slate. Kontakt rows follow the same rule: label 15 / 500 mute, value Inter 600 at lead size. Step numbers are navy Fraunces, not coral. A coral-ink label above a card title is 15 / 500. Round arrow controls: white, hairline, navy glyph, sky on hover.
  • Footer is a navy band. Band spacing (56 → 96 above, 32 → 48 below), blocks 32 → 48 apart. Two text colours: white for the name (Fraunces 19 / 500, like the logo, on every page) and headings, #C9D8E3 for everything else (8.1 : 1). Every text link is a 44 px target. Left aligned; no logo image (the name is the logo). No crisis note (owner removed it, 12 Nov); the same footer on every page, Kontakt column included (owner, 13 Nov); Facebook and Instagram are two 44 px round hairline controls, the first starting at the column's left edge; on desktop the bottom line has © on the left and the privacy link on the right.
  • Ceník uses the same parts (lab: ui-lab/proposal/cenik.html). Hero on paper; the two prices are cards in the format tints, sky and peach (title names the format: "Online", "Osobně v Ostravě", h3; price at h2 size, facts as a closed-up list, full-width button); Praktické informace is its own sky band with rows, no lines (three columns from 769 px); První setkání is the peach booking panel from the homepage. No uppercase labels, tick marks, shadows or cards on cards.
  • Review cards cycle through three palette colours only: blue (white text), a light one, white with a hairline. The light one is sky on a paper band and paper on a sky band (the homepage has Recenze on sky). Name only, no details.
  • No entrance or scroll animations. Motion only as feedback (FAQ opening, menu, hover colour), at most 200 ms, and none when the visitor asks for reduced motion.

Page rhythm

Bands of paper and sky, not white and beige

Same spacing tokens as today (they work). Section headers left-aligned on desktop, centred on phones (your earlier call), no subtitle unless it says something specific.

Paper

Hero, Pro koho, Služby, Osobní a online, Členství

Sky

O mně, Recenze, Jak probíhá, FAQ, Kontakt

Navy

Footer only, solid — no gradient. The booking panel is peach.

  • Break the equal grids: "Pro koho" as a short list with one sentence each, not 4 identical cards; "Osobní a online" as two columns (online / Ostrava) instead of 4 benefit cards.
  • Portrait: the blob shape stays as the one exception, but it no longer morphs (no decorative loop).

Language

Kristýna's voice, consistently

Rules

  • "vy" in lowercase everywhere (vám, vaše). Today 9× capital, 12× lowercase on the homepage alone.
  • "já", not "my" — it's a one-person practice. "společně najdeme" → "najdeme spolu" is fine, "upravujeme" → "upravím".
  • One fact beats one adjective. Price, length, place, method (daseinsanalýza), who it's for.
  • No subtitle by default under section titles.

Add, remove, keep

Add

  • Review sources as one line under the cards, linking to the profiles. (A footer crisis note was tried and removed by the owner, 12 Nov.)
  • Method in one sentence (daseinsanalýza) — what a session actually feels like.

Remove

  • Playfair Display.
  • All 7 gradients.
  • Title underline, coloured side bars.
  • Card shadows; most uppercase labels.
  • 6 off-palette colours.
  • Filler subtitles; italic reviews.

Keep

  • Coral booking buttons.
  • Blog and quiz illustrations.
  • Real portrait and room photo.
  • Prices and terms up front.
  • Credentials and supervisors.
  • 17 px text, 66-char lines, spacing tokens, no trackers.
