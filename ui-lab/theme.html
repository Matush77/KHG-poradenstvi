KHG theme sheet

What the live site actually uses today, measured in the browser on 12 Nov 2026 on the homepage, blog, one article, a quiz and the price list, at 1440 px and 375 px. Everything below renders with the site's own CSS and fonts.

The older guide ui-lab/VISUAL-LANGUAGE.md describes the site before the polish layer went live (commit 8bcf379), so its §1–§9 numbers are outdated. §10 there is close to what is live now.

1 · Fonts: 2 families

Playfair Display

Serif, self-hosted variable file, weights 600–700. Used for page and section titles, panel titles, blog card titles, prices. Only weight 700 is actually used. Digits are old-style (uneven heights) — use Inter for numbers that must line up.

Inter

Sans-serif, self-hosted variable file, weights 300–700. Used for everything else: body, card titles, labels, buttons, navigation, FAQ.

Weights in use

400 Regular — body, leadsmost text
500 Medium — navigation, dates, bylinecommon
600 Semibold — buttons, links, author namescommon
700 Bold — card titles, labels, FAQ, all Playfaircommon
650 and 900 — asked for in CSS on a few strong/linksstray

900 can't render (the font stops at 700), so it shows as 700. 650 shows as an in-between weight on 2 places only. Both should be 600 or 700.

7 · Language and voice

What it is

  • Czech, formal "vykání", first person singular from Kristýna ("Pracuji…", "Nabízím…").
  • Calm, reassuring, plain — short sentences, no jargon, no pressure ("Nemusíte se nijak zvlášť připravovat").
  • Practical facts stated plainly: 50 minut, 1 100 Kč, 36 hodin storno.

Where it wobbles drift

  • "Vám / vám" both used. Homepage: 9× capital "Vám, Vaše", 12× lowercase "vám, vašich". Price list only capital, blog only lowercase. Pick one (lowercase is the modern web norm).
  • Generic filler next to personal text: "Připraveni začít?", "Pomáhám lidem v různých životních situacích", one subtitle under every section title.
  • "I" vs "we": mostly "já", but "společně najdeme", "upravujeme", "abychom mohli" switch to "my".

8 · Summary

Consistent now keep

  • 2 font families with clear roles.
  • Core palette: blue + coral + warm neutrals.
  • Homepage type scale H1–label.
  • Radii 10 / 16 / 24 / pill.
  • White / cream section alternation, fluid spacing.
  • Coral pill buttons, 2 sizes.
  • No pictograms; arrows only.

Not consistent yet fix

  • Blog, article, quiz and price-list headings use their own sizes (20, 21, 22, 30, 36, 48, 52).
  • Body text 15 / 16 / 17 / 18 used interchangeably.
  • Weights 650 / 900 in CSS.
  • 3 cool tints left (FAQ, image backdrops, related articles).
  • Italic centred reviews (being replaced in the lab).
  • "Vám" vs "vám"; "já" vs "my".
  • Gold logo isn't part of the palette at all.
